KHOJ prototype v4

Refined iOS style: glass materials, spring motion, Find My style precision screen. Drag the list up and down on the radar. Everything moves by itself; all names and numbers are sample data.

  • Radar: a safe-zone disc, your heading cone, people as bubbles, far people pinned to the edge with an arrow
  • Sonar pulses, heading-up rotation, springy draggable sheet
  • Find: colour-adaptive screen, rolling numbers, pulse when you point straight at them
  • Group is a pass, like a Wallet card
  • New: Voice. Mic button on Nearby (hold-to-talk commands), "Say a message" on Activity (dictation), a spoken line on Find, and Voice settings on Group. Tap a phrase chip on the voice screen to try it.
  • New: Light up. From Find, tap Light up to ask a family phone to turn on its torch, bright screen and sound. Open "Lit phone" to see what the other person sees. The consent switch is on the Group tab.
