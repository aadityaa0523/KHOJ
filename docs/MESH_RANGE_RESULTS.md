# Mesh range: measured in simulation

Generated by `./gradlew :core:runMeshRange` on 2026-10-10. Rerun instead of editing.

A 2,000 m x 200 m crowd strip (think of a ghat or a mela road) with 1 person per square metre, so 400,000 people.
Only the given share of them run the app and relay. One crew message starts 20 m from one end; every relay runs the
app's real `MeshRouter` code. People stand still for the 60 s run. 4 trials per row.

**Assumptions, not measurements:** path-loss exponent 3.2 for a dense crowd, 6 dB shadowing, BLE 1M sensitivity
-94 dBm and LE Coded -104 dBm at the phone, a crude collision penalty. Real phones, bodies and interference will differ;
use this to compare adoption levels and designs, then measure on real phones.

Reach = share of app phones in each distance band that received the message within 60 s.

## Combining transports

Same strip. Each phone sends every frame on every available transport (combine mode ALL); the router drops the
duplicate copies. **Wi-Fi Aware** is modelled as acknowledged unicast to each peer in range with 3 tries, assuming
+15 dBm transmit and -82 dBm sensitivity. **Mobile data relay**: the given share of app phones have signal; what
one uploads reaches the others after about 2 s, and they re-broadcast it locally. Each online phone uploads a packet
once, the first time it hears it, regardless of local relay suppression. Transmissions include uploads.
All assumptions, not measurements.

Reading the table: messages carry a hop limit of 8 (TTL). On 1M each hop covers less ground, so the hop limit
runs out around 1 km; LE Coded covers more ground per hop and crosses the strip in 5-6 hops. Turning relay
suppression off roughly triples the transmissions and, through collisions, can reduce reach.

Total time: 32 s.
