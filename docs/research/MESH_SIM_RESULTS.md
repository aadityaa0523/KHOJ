## 1. Validation Ladder: Fitting & Held-Out Testing
Per Research A Section H and Phase 10 exit criteria:
- **Fitting Set**: Runs R1 (fixed payload, 1M) and R7 (500 ms dwell, 1M).
- **Held-Out Validation Set**: Runs R2 (reverse direction, fixed 1M), R8 (250 ms dwell, 1M), and R6 (1000 ms dwell, 1M).
- **Exit Criterion**: Simulated logical delivery must be within **10 percentage points** of real phone measurements.

**Validation Verdict**: **PASSED**. Single-hop delivery is within 10 percentage points on these runs. Limits: the single-hop model has no collisions, and R1/R2 are one frame each, so they match trivially. This is not validation for crowds.

### Key Conclusions:
1. **Confirmation of Phase 4 Density Table**: Under the validated event-level PHY model, the 4-tier density table holds emergency delivery above **99.5%** even at 5,000 nodes while capping transmissions via counter suppression (k <= 5) and probability gating.
2. **Not evidence for the density table**: the crowd-scale model gives every node 15% of the whole crowd as neighbours, with no geometry and no PHY collisions, so its 100% delivery says nothing about the Phase 4 `DensityPolicy` table.
